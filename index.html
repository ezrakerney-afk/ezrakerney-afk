About me

An aspiring engineer who loves to build.

I'm a 16-year-old junior at Gettysburg Area High School with my sights set on engineering. I'm drawn to the process of identifying a problem, designing a solution, and refining it until it works. Computer science is one branch of that interest, alongside math and hands-on design, and I program in Java. I'm also a strong language learner: I'm nearly fluent in Spanish and speak French well.

I put that mindset into practice by independently creating GAHSgames.com, developing the Roblox game Altus, and I keep sharpening it through the Technology Student Association, the National Math Honors Society, and Math Club, and as a member of Hack Club, a global community of teen builders, I compete in game jams. Beyond STEM, I've built my public speaking and argumentation skills through Speech and Debate and Mock Trial, served my community through Key Club, and I'm a member of French Club. I compete on the varsity tennis team, play Ultimate Frisbee, raced mountain bikes in the Pennsylvania Interscholastic Cycling League (PICL) for six seasons, and was in Chess Club for three years. My jobs in retail and food service have taught me reliability, teamwork, and how to perform under pressure. I'm also well traveled, and I make a point of learning about the culture of each place I visit.

● Live Short
tutorial
Full-stack web app · Accounts, quizzes & leaderboard

Shortutorial.com

Short, clear tutorials for anything. Visitors can search the catalog, browse by category, or jump to a random tutorial. The step-by-step fix guides cover everyday computer problems, like Wi-Fi that's connected with no internet, an offline printer, a full drive, a slow computer, or a second monitor that isn't detected, with a Windows/Mac switch and a built-in troubleshooter. There's also a programming crash course on JCurses for building colorful terminal programs in Java.

Every tutorial ends with a certification quiz. Visitors can create an account, earn certifications, and compete on a world leaderboard. Quiz answers are checked on the server so they can't be read from the page.

● Roblox Altus
Multiplayer game · Designed & developed in Roblox Studio

Altus

A fast-paced 4v4 first-person shooter on Roblox with Capture the Flag and Deathmatch modes across ten handcrafted maps, from a Japanese town to a storm-swept harbor and jungle ruins. Matches feature a ranked ladder, unlockable abilities, weapon skins and knives, a 60-tier season pass, and an animated MVP showcase at the end of every round. Bots fill empty slots so every match is a full 4v4, and real players take their places as they join.

I designed the game and led its development from the ground up in Roblox Studio, writing the systems in Luau with the help of AI-assisted development tools and refining them through repeated playtesting. That work included server-validated hit detection, an Elo-based ranking system where the top tier is reserved for the top 10% of players, skill-based matchmaking between servers, a full in-game economy with disclosed case odds, persistent player data, and touch controls for mobile. I also built a private developer dashboard for live game settings, a cross-server lobby browser, player management, and gifting.
