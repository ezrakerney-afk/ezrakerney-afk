Live Solo project · Designed & built independently

GAHSgames.com

A student-created platform where students can share games they've built with each other, whether for a class project or just for fun. The site gives student developers a place to showcase their ideas, learn from one another's work, and get inspired for their next project.

I planned, built, and launched the entire site on my own, including the game catalog, a highscores page, feedback and contact pages, and site policies.
